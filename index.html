How Caseboard works

Three opening clues start the case, each naming a category and a count — WOOD, 4 — meaning that many words on the board fit it. Tap any word you think belongs to the hidden set of eight. A correct tap stays revealed and your turn continues, up to one guess past the clue's count. A wrong tap ends your turn immediately and moves you to the next clue. You can also proceed early to bank your hits and move on.

Once the opening clues are used, any word you haven't found yet gets its own one-word follow-up clue — but only the words still outstanding; anything you already found is skipped automatically. If you still haven't found everything after every follow-up clue, the board stays open for free guessing until you do — there's no instant fail, you can always finish.

Your score is clues used, plus one point per miss, plus two per Red Herring miss — lower is better. Everyone gets this same board and the same clue order, so the only edge is category logic, not timing.

A new case unlocks every day. Restart to try today's case again from the start.
